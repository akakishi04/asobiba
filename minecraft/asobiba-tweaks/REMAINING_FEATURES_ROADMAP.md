## Implementation closure and publication gates

The bounded accepted implementation scopes are complete in the round7 local snapshot. No human gameplay or final publication result is implied.

1. **Bounded resource sites:** fixed Forestry/Quarry/Fishing assignments, physical demand/reserves, real matching saplings, non-overlapping replacement quarries and delayed same-boundary forestry reapproval are integrated; all twelve tests passed. Historical natural-looking player resource blocks still rely on conservative heuristics before tracked placement provenance exists.
2. **Shared dynamic boundary/history:** the 48→128-block connected activity reference, safe indexed terminal-history maintenance and reservation release are implemented; all eleven tests passed. Durable blueprints/referenced ownership remain exempt from ephemeral limits, oversized legacy catalogs fail closed, and optional abandonment compaction is not required for this bounded pass. See `VILLAGE_BOUNDARY_HISTORY_NOTES.md` for implementation details; its older verification checkpoint is superseded here.
3. **Contextual mob building use:** bounded idle-only interior/shelter, campfire/furniture and real reachable route preferences are implemented; all nine tests passed. Immediate roof cover uses physical blocks rather than delayed sky-light state.
4. **Physical rivers:** both real chunk-carving scenarios passed, including seams/lake/navigation and lowered-terrain/native guards after replacing stale `WORLD_SURFACE_WG` with live `WORLD_SURFACE`.
5. **Publication:** both local builds and the identical-source full-suite repeat passed. Verify dedicated-server smoke and CI for the exact published integration hash before conditional merge. Local PASS alone is not that gate.

## Accepted future small oddities (not yet implemented)

- **Chicken Conspiracy / 鶏の密談 — PLANNED:** Occasionally, three or more nearby chickens stand briefly facing the same direction as if conspiring, then disperse naturally when a player approaches. Purely atmospheric and unrewarded. Implement with an independent config toggle, finite loaded-entity checks and no interference with essential chicken AI. See `../MOD_IDEAS.md` (World oddities).
- **O01 ambient event expansion — PLANNED:** Seven harmless new events, OE01 Replying Bell, OE02 Unseen Footsteps, OE03 Empty Knock, OE04 Cold Ember, OE05 Unusual Glance, OE06 Backward Breeze and OE07 Unclaimed Chord. Each may naturally happen very rarely even without O01; O01 only increases already-eligible occurrence within hard rate/loaded-chunk/AI constraints. Eligible Chicken Conspiracy, Mob Gatherings and N20 Nod may also be biased under their own independent prerequisites. No hazardous events, extra rewards, fake entities or world edits.

## Accepted future enchantments (not yet implemented)

- **L01 — 根付き / Rooted (Hoe) — PLANNED:** Harvest eligible mature crops and replant in the vacated safe cell using a genuinely owned matching seed. No duplication, free planting or area harvesting.
- **O01 — 不吉 / Ominous (Armor) — PLANNED:** Slightly bias harmless eligible ambient encounters in the expanded OE01–OE07 pool and separately permitted oddities; never Bad Omen, dangerous/hostile events, increased loot or N17 combat behavior. Multi-piece scaling must be capped.
- **N17 — 残像 / Afterimage Decoy (Chestplate) — PLANNED:** On a genuine sprint departure, briefly create a translucent afterimage that can draw the attention of a small bounded number of nearby ordinary hostile mobs. Proposed duration ~1.5 seconds, cooldown ~15 seconds; no bosses, PvP, dangerous permanent aggro modifications, free drops or persistent phantom target.
- **N20 — 頷き / Nod (Helmet) — PLANNED:** Greet an idle villager by facing them and crouching; they may briefly nod. Purely social, including children, no reputation/trade benefits; O01 affects eligibility only when N20 is actually worn.
- All four enchantments and the OE01–OE07 event family are **accepted future work, not implemented**. Enchantment registry, levels, effects, client/server sync, configuration and GameTests remain to be built. They are not part of the historical 224-test v0.1 closure. Detailed decisions and safety limits: `../MOD_IDEAS.md`.

## Working policy

1. Complete accepted missing behavior and fix failing conservation/runtime checks. User-delegated design allows bounded choices; it does not turn every future idea into a release requirement.
2. Keep real resources authoritative, preserve player edits/cargo, never force-load chunks or manufacture recovery items, and keep river cargo default ON.
3. Preserve historical test evidence as historical. Record exact final hashes and actual completed test results before claiming publication, CI PASS or merge eligibility.
4. Treat real-client bug reports as a high-priority correction queue. Keep gameplay polish and future breadth visible without labeling implemented systems absent.
5. **Minecraft Data Logger remains explicitly deferred.** Conditional merge requires the requested implementation scope and checks to be complete; no merge approval is inferred from this roadmap.
